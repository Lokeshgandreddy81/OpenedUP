# Slice 03 — Sharing and release review

## Contract

An owner can share with signed-in Shoebox users and revoke access. Recipients can open and export but cannot edit, delete, restore, or re-share the artifact. Signed-out visitors cannot list or retrieve shared artifacts.

## Checkpoint

Review authenticated route middleware and owner predicates; exercise owner and recipient screens with two signed-in accounts when credentials are available. Confirm no public link is exposed. Finish with frontend/backend type checks and builds; record separately any live-account and browser-only verification that cannot run in this environment.
